GOOD TO KNOW / 07

Questions,
answered.

How do I know which cover fits my car? +

Choose your make, model, and year in the Cover Lab or Find My Fit. The final measurements and fit are confirmed with you before production.

Can I order a personalized design? +

Yes. The Cover Lab lets you explore colors, graphic styles, a name or initials, and text placement. The visual preview is illustrative and final artwork is confirmed before production.

What materials and protection levels are available? +

Standard, Premium, and Signature are example tiers in this prototype. Confirm the exact fabric, weather suitability, and care instructions with the seller before ordering.

How do delivery, payment, and returns work? +

These policies are being finalized. Contact the brand for current delivery areas, payment options, lead times, and return terms before placing an order.
